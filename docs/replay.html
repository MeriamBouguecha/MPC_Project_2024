About this simulation & credits

MATLAB simulation results from the EPFL ME-425 team project by Niklas Schröder, MeriamBouguecha, and jessiestaudenmann. Linear MPC uses YALMIP; nonlinear MPC uses CasADi/IPOPT with a 10 s horizon. The lead vehicle is projected at constant speed. Lateral scaling is enlarged for legibility.
