About this simulation & credits

These trajectories were computed with the recovered team controllers from niklashcs/mpc_epfl (Niklas Schröder, Mariem Bougacha, and Jessie Staudenmann). The linear scenarios use the recovered YALMIP formulations and longitudinal observer. The nonlinear controllers keep the original CasADi/IPOPT formulation, 10 s horizon, objective weights, and RK4 model. The avoidance version corrects an extra state/input allocation so every constrained state is connected to the dynamics. The lead vehicle is projected at constant speed inside the optimizer. The replay is computed telemetry; it does not run an optimizer in the browser. Lateral display scaling is enlarged for legibility. The ellipse is a modeled separation constraint, not a certified collision avoidance guarantee. The accompanying source manifest, cleanup notes, and validation report describe provenance and tested limits.
